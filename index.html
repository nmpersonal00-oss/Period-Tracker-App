Room for intention and reflection

What is a purity day? A day you personally recognized that menstruation had ended, according to the signs of purity and guidance you follow. You can note that day here; you do not need to check off every day between periods. You can separately record ghusl and write an ibadah reflection. Rosie never decides religious purity for you.

Helpful source

Religious rulings concerning menstruation and purity can involve details that a calendar cannot infer. Review the sources you trust or ask a qualified scholar for your circumstances.
